Pixel Brick Grid Support

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pixel Brick Grid handles information when you use the game. Pixel Brick Grid is a single-player puzzle game with local progress saving, background music settings, and advertising and security SDK integrations.

Information Stored on Your Device

The game stores gameplay progress locally on your device, including unlocked levels, star ratings, scores, and music settings. This local information is used to restore your progress and preferences. The game does not require an account.

Tracking Permission and Advertising

The app may request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. If you allow tracking, the app may access the advertising identifier to support advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and ad performance analysis. If you deny permission, the app can still be used.

Third-Party SDKs

The app may use third-party security, advertising, and measurement SDKs. These SDKs may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers when permitted, IP address, device information, app information, ad interaction data, and diagnostic information according to their own privacy practices.

Network Access

The app may use network access to initialize security services, load advertising configuration, request advertisements, and support measurement or fraud prevention. Gameplay progress itself is saved locally.

Data We Do Not Collect Directly

  • We do not require a player account.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or payment details inside the game.
  • We do not sell personal information directly through the game.

Children's Privacy

The app is intended for a general audience. If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information through third-party services connected to the app, please contact us so we can review the concern.

Your Choices

You can manage tracking permission in iOS Settings. You can also delete the app to remove locally stored game progress and settings from your device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any update will be reflected by changing the effective date on this page.
