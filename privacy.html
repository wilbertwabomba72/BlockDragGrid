Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pixel Brick Grid handles information when you use the game. Pixel Brick Grid is a single-player block puzzle game with local progress saving, music settings, and advertising, measurement, and security SDK integrations.

Information Stored Locally

The game stores gameplay progress on your device, including unlocked levels, star ratings, scores, and music settings. This information is used to restore your progress and preferences. The game does not require a player account.

Tracking Permission

The app may request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. If you allow tracking, the app may access the advertising identifier to support advertising, measurement, ad performance analysis, and fraud prevention. If you deny permission, you can still play the game.

Third-Party SDKs

The app may use third-party advertising, measurement, and security SDKs. These SDKs may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers when permitted, IP address, device information, app information, ad interaction data, diagnostic data, and fraud prevention signals according to their own privacy practices.

Network Access

Pixel Brick Grid may use network access to initialize security services, load advertising configuration, request advertisements, and support measurement or fraud prevention. Gameplay progress itself is saved locally on your device.

Information We Do Not Request

  • The game does not require an account.
  • The game does not ask for your name, phone number, payment details, or mailing address.
  • The game does not require you to upload gameplay progress to an online account.

Your Choices

You can manage tracking permission in iOS Settings. You can delete the app to remove locally stored game progress and settings from your device.

Children's Privacy

Pixel Brick Grid is intended for a general audience. If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information through services connected to the app, please contact us through the support contact listed on the App Store product page.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any update will be reflected by changing the effective date on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please use the support contact listed on the App Store product page.
