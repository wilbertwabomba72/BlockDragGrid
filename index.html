Pixel Brick Grid

Clear lines, collect crystals, and solve colorful block puzzle levels across four themed worlds.

  • 50 Levels
  • 8x8 Board
  • Four Themes
  • Bomb Tool

Play With Purpose

Clear Lines

Place pieces on an 8x8 board and complete full rows or columns to open space and score.

Chase Goals

Each level has a target score, star rating, and rising challenge curve built for quick puzzle sessions.

Collect Crystals

Later stages add crystals, locked bricks, and blueprint pads so every board asks for a plan.

Use Tools

Spend a Bomb to blast a 3x3 area or refresh the candidate pool when the board gets tight.

Four Worlds

Play through Neon Lab, Crystal Vault, Brick Factory, and Jelly Bay with matching art and effects.

Local Progress

Unlock levels, save stars, and toggle music locally. No account is required to play.
